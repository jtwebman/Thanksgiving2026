Here's our Thanksgiving plan:

**What we're bringing (enough for 12–15 people)**
1. Turkey: both turkeys, cut up, butter-injected, and roasted in pans so they stay juicy
2. Mashed potatoes
3. Sweet potato casserole with butter, brown sugar, and marshmallows
4. Stove Top stuffing (we already have the 2 boxes)
5. Frog eye salad

**Schedule**
- **Mon 11/23 (tonight):** Grocery run
- **Wed 11/25:** Cut up, season, and roast the turkeys. Slice the meat and store it in pans with the juices. Start the frog eye salad (pasta and custard need to chill overnight).
- **Thu 11/26 morning:** Finish the frog eye salad, make the mashed potatoes and the sweet potato base, and reheat the turkey in our oven.
- **Thu 2:00 pm:** Leave our house (30-minute drive).
- **At their house, 2:30–4:00:** The turkey and sweet potatoes stay warm in their oven, the marshmallows go on the sweet potatoes, and we make the Stove Top fresh on their stove.
- **4:00 pm:** Dinner!

**Things to bring Thursday:** the turkey and sweet potato pans (in the cooler with towels), the marshmallow topping, the crockpot of mashed potatoes plus its cord, the Stove Top kit (2 boxes, broth, butter, and a pot), the frog eye salad (with ice packs), serving spoons, oven mitts, and the thermometer.
