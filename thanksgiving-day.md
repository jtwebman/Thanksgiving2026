## Turkey: reheat
1. **11:45:** pour **1–1½ cups chicken broth** over each pan and add a few pats of butter. Cover **tightly** with foil.
2. **12:15:** bake at **350°F**, **1 pan per rack**, for **50–60 min**, until the thickest piece reads **165°F** on the thermometer. Swap the pans between racks halfway through.
3. **Drive:** keep the foil on, wrap the pans in towels, and put them in the cooler.
4. **At the host's house:** hold the pans at **250°F**, covered, from 2:30 to 3:10. Check for **165°F**, then keep them covered in foil and towels until dinner. If it looks dry, add a splash of broth.

## Mashed Potatoes (about 45 min, serves 12–15)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Yukon Gold potatoes | 7 lb |
| Butter | 1½ sticks (¾ cup) |
| Half-and-half or whole milk, warmed | 1½–2 cups |
| Cream cheese, softened | 8 oz |
| Kosher salt | about 1½ Tbsp (to taste) |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp |

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 2" chunks. Put them in a big pot with **cold** water covering them by an inch, plus a big pinch of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer for **15–20 min**, until a fork slides in with no resistance.
3. Drain well and put them back in the hot pot for 1–2 min to steam off the extra water.
4. Mash with the butter and cream cheese, then mix in the warm half-and-half a little at a time until they're as creamy as you like. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Move them to a **buttered crockpot on Warm**. The cream cheese keeps them creamy for hours. Stir in a splash of milk before serving if needed.

## Sweet Potatoes (roasted Wednesday night, finished at the host's house)
They're already roasted with butter and brown sugar (see the Wednesday page). Thursday you just reheat them and add the marshmallows.

| Bring | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pan of roasted sweet potatoes, cold from the fridge and covered | 1 (9x13) |
| Mini marshmallows | 1 bag (10 oz) |

1. **Drive:** keep them cold, in the bag with the frog eye salad and ice packs. At the host's house, put them in the fridge.
2. **3:10:** bake them **covered** at **350°F** for **20–25 min**, until they're hot all the way through.
3. **3:35:** uncover them, gently stir so the chunks get coated in the buttery brown sugar syrup, and cover the top with an even layer of **marshmallows**.
4. Bake **10–15 min**, until the marshmallows are puffed and golden. Watch them closely at the end.

## Stove Top Stuffing (10 min, made at the host's house)
| Bring | Amount |
|---|---|
| Stove Top | 2 boxes (6 oz each) |
| Chicken broth (instead of water, for more flavor) | 3 cups |
| Butter | 1 stick (½ cup) |
| A medium pot with a lid | in case they don't have a spare |

1. Bring the broth and butter to a boil.
2. Stir in both boxes of stuffing mix, cover, and **take the pot off the heat**. Let it sit for **5 min**.
3. Fluff it with a fork and serve.

## Gravy: reheat (10 min, at the host's house)
1. Scrape the gravy into a small pot. It will be a jelly from the fridge; that's normal.
2. Warm it over **medium-low heat**, whisking, until it's smooth and bubbling, about 5–10 min.
3. Whisk in a splash of broth if it's too thick.

## Packet Gravy (5 min, at the host's house)
For anyone who prefers the packet kind. 4 packets make about 4 cups.
1. Whisk the 4 packets into the amount of **cold water the package says** in a small pot. Starting with cold water keeps it from getting lumpy.
2. Bring it to a boil over medium heat, whisking. Simmer for 1 min until it thickens.
3. Keep it on low until you serve it. Put it in its own bowl or gravy boat so nobody mixes it up with the homemade gravy.

## Frog Eye Salad, Part 2 (15 min, at 10:00)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pasta base from Wednesday | the whole bowl |
| Pineapple (drained Wednesday) | 2 cans' worth |
| Fruit cocktail, drained | 2 cans (15 oz) |
| Mandarin oranges, drained | 2 cans (11 oz) |
| Maraschino cherries, drained and halved | 1 jar (10 oz); save a few whole ones for the top |
| Cool Whip, thawed | 2 tubs (8 oz) |
| Mini marshmallows | 2 cups |
| Sweetened shredded coconut | 1 cup |

1. **Drain the canned fruit really well** so the salad doesn't get watery. Pat the cherries dry.
2. Gently fold everything into the pasta base.
3. Top with a few whole cherries and a sprinkle of coconut. Cover and refrigerate.
4. **Drive:** take it in a bag with ice packs, **not** in the cooler with the hot pans.
