# Shopping List (Mon 11/23)

**Already have:** 2 turkeys (thawing in the fridge since Sat), 2 boxes of Stove Top, lots of butter (the whole menu uses about 10 sticks), a meat injector, an oven-safe meat thermometer, a crockpot, and a cooler with towels.

**Check the pantry first.** Most people already have the items marked (P).

## Produce
- [ ] 7 lb Yukon Gold potatoes (mashed)
- [ ] 5 lb sweet potatoes (about 7–8 medium)
- [ ] 2 yellow onions (for the turkey pans)
- [ ] 1 bunch celery (for the turkey pans)
- [ ] 1 head garlic (for the turkey pans)
- [ ] 1 lemon, or use bottled lemon juice (P)
- [ ] Fresh thyme or rosemary, 1 pack (optional, for the turkey pans)

## Dairy / Refrigerated
- [ ] Half-and-half or whole milk, 1 quart (mashed potatoes and sweet potatoes)
- [ ] 8 oz cream cheese, 1 block (helps the mashed potatoes stay creamy while they're held hot)
- [ ] Eggs, half dozen (2 for frog eye salad, 2 for sweet potatoes)
- [ ] Cool Whip, two 8 oz tubs (frozen aisle; thaw in the fridge Wednesday night)

## Canned / Jarred (frog eye salad)
- [ ] Pineapple tidbits or chunks in juice, 2 cans (20 oz). Save the juice.
- [ ] Pineapple juice, 1 small can (6 oz) or a 6-pack, as backup if the cans don't give 1¾ cups
- [ ] Fruit cocktail in juice, 2 cans (15 oz)
- [ ] Mandarin oranges, 2 cans (11 oz)
- [ ] Maraschino cherries, 1 jar (10 oz)

## Dry Goods
- [ ] Acini di pepe pasta, 1 lb (pasta aisle, often Barilla or Ronzoni). If you can't find it, get pastina or orzo.
- [ ] Mini marshmallows, 2 bags (10 oz): 1 for the sweet potatoes, 1 for the salad
- [ ] Sweetened shredded coconut, 1 bag (7 oz)
- [ ] Low-sodium chicken broth, 5 cartons (32 oz): injection, turkey pans, gravy, reheating, and the Stove Top
- [ ] Brown sugar, 1 bag (2 lb), unless you already have plenty
- [ ] Sugar (P), flour (P; about ¾ cup total for the gravy and salad), vanilla (P)

## Spices (for the turkey rub and injection; check what you have)
- [ ] Kosher salt (P)
- [ ] Black pepper (P)
- [ ] Garlic powder
- [ ] Onion powder
- [ ] Smoked paprika
- [ ] Dried thyme
- [ ] Rubbed sage
- [ ] Dried rosemary
- [ ] Cinnamon and nutmeg (sweet potatoes)

## Supplies
- [ ] Disposable aluminum roasting pans, **6–8 full-size**: 4 to roast the turkey, 2–3 to store and reheat it, plus spares
- [ ] 1 disposable 9x13 pan, if you don't want to send your own dish for the sweet potatoes
- [ ] Heavy-duty foil, 1 large roll
- [ ] Gallon zip bags and disposable gloves (handy for breaking down the turkeys)
- [ ] Ice packs, if you don't have any (to keep the frog eye salad cold on the drive)
